Executable V2 format: output one complete compact JSON object only.
Top-level fields: formatVersion=2, title, summary (one sentence), inviter, participants (1–3 candidate internal NPC names), locationId, timeSlot, mailBody, beats.
Creative suggestions, not acceptance gates: prefer two choices with two options each and 10–18 beats. Use concise dialogue, roughly one short sentence per line/response. The final responseText can conclude the story. Dialogue count, addressee, spacing and use of when are creative decisions. Do not force all three ending labels.
Execution contract: hard cap of 48 beats. At least one choice; at least two complete options per choice, up to a technical capacity of 48. More than three options are paginated, never discarded. These limits are not targets. Do not indent or emit empty/default fields, default durationMs, empty choices or memoryText.
type is enter, move, face, animation, emote, dialogue, pause, camera, sound, choice, exit or end. actor is a participating internal NPC name, never player. target is player, player_left, player_right, center, left, right, empty, or a participating internal NPC name. Map IDs belong only in locationId. camera targets player or a participant.
animation/emote cue is empty, wave, sit, look_away, cheer, shy, surprised, sad, happy or love. sound cue is empty, soft, surprise, mystery, storm or footstep.
Every choice is unconditional with a unique id, e.g. c1/c2. Every option has id (unique within that node), text (player choice), responseActor, distinct responseText and outcome.
Ordinary beats and options may have when, e.g. {"c1":"stay"}. Multiple keys mean AND; absent/{} means unconditional. Refer only to existing options in earlier choices. A choice beat itself cannot have when. No cycles, forward references, next/goto/flags. Every reachable route must have at least two available options at each choice.
outcome is romantic, warm, playful, honest, shared, neutral or distant. intimacy defaults to none; affection, kiss and private describe the selected narrative, not heart tiers. boundary defaults to false and records narrative meaning without implicitly hiding options. Respect character background and the player's actual choices.
Every option in the last choice has ending: closer, unresolved or apart; labels may repeat and not all are required. Earlier options omit ending. The sole end must be the final unconditional beat. Code settles relationship changes once on completion; output no numeric rewards.
